Privacy notice

For the personal ClearSignal screening tool

Searches and information

ClearSignal does not require an account and does not save search names to a ClearSignal database. Recent searches are held in browser memory for the current session only.

When you run a search, your entered name, selected publisher country, and adverse-news search terms are sent directly from your browser to the GDELT DOC news search service. If you choose the Google News fallback, the search phrase is sent to Google News. Those third-party services may handle requests under their own terms and privacy practices.

Hosting and browser data

The hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs such as IP address and request details. ClearSignal does not use advertising, analytics, or payment tools.

Use responsibly

Do not search confidential information unless you are authorized to share it with the named news services. A name match is not identity verification or proof of wrongdoing. Review original reporting before making decisions.
