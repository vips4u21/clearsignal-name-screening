Privacy notice

For the personal ClearSignal screening tool

Searches and information

ClearSignal does not require an account and does not save search names to a ClearSignal database. Recent searches are held in browser memory for the current session only.

When you run a search, your entered name and adverse-news terms are sent to Google News. Your selected publisher country is used to set the Google News region. Google may handle requests under its own terms and privacy practices.

Hosting and browser data

The hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs such as IP address and request details. ClearSignal does not use advertising, analytics, or payment tools.

Use responsibly

Do not search confidential information unless you are authorized to share it with Google News. A name match is not identity verification or proof of wrongdoing. Review original reporting before making decisions.
